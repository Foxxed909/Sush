# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who live in the terminal and run AI coding agents (Claude Code,
Codex, Gemini) as a daily crew — often several at once, often on modest
hardware, sometimes on battery, sometimes offline. They are fluent in tools
like Warp, iTerm2, Linear, and Raycast, and they are allergic to cloud
accounts, telemetry, and being upsold mid-task. Context: deep in a coding
task, orchestrating agents, watching quotas.

## Product Purpose

Sush is "the terminal your agents live in": one place to launch, brief,
monitor, and hand off AI agent sessions — with multi-account limit rotation,
usage guarding, voice dictation, and full offline operation. Success = the
user ships work through their agents without ever being stranded by a rate
limit or babysitting a swarm.

## Brand Personality

Confident, quiet, a little playful. A hacker tool with taste: dark, pink-
accented, glass used sparingly. It whispers (Sush/Hush/Quiet Credits — the
naming is a shush motif), never shouts.

## Anti-references

- Warp's cloud-account gating and telemetry-first posture.
- SaaS-landing slop: gradient text, hero metrics, identical feature-card
  walls, eyebrow labels over every section.
- Electron apps that feel like websites: laggy, mouse-first, battery-blind.

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears into the task** — terminals are the content; chrome
   recedes.
2. **Never strand the user** — every limit, error, or dead end offers the
   next move (switch account, hand off, resume).
3. **Honest metering** — quotas and credits are shown in real units, locally,
   with no dark patterns.
4. **Battery is a feature** — every ornament has an off-switch; polling is
   focus-gated.
5. **Keyboard-first, voice-close** — everything reachable by chord or palette;
   dictation one key away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduce-motion honoured twice (OS preference + explicit toggle). Body text
targets ≥4.5:1 contrast on the dark surfaces. Full keyboard reachability.
Dictation serves users who type less. No color-only state signals — states
pair color with label text.
