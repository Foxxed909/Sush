## Phase 4 decisions (owner)

1. **Usage chrome:** not always-on bars in the status strip. One **circle control** (and Settings ▸ Usage). Click shows:
   - **Total accounts (N): sum of remaining %** (e.g. 4 accounts at ~100 each → **400%**)
   - Then each account on its own row: `Account · ~100`
2. **Example language:** do not hard-code “N Free accounts ≈ N00%” marketing numbers in docs or success criteria — those were illustrative only.
